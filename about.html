Our Story

We don't believe in rushed schedules.
We believe in journeys that feel real.

Founded 2020
Travellers Guided 5,000+
Destinations 45+
Rooted Locally Terrain, micro-climates & mountain communities
Certified Safety Wilderness First Aid (WFR) & mountain emergency protocols
Ethical Tourism Leave No Trace & local economic empowerment

Shivayan Trails started not in a corporate boardroom, but around high-altitude campfires and along steep ridge trails in the Garhwal Himalayas.

We saw travel becoming transactional — crowded buses, hurried itineraries, and travellers returning exhausted rather than refreshed. We wanted to build something different: journeys where you breathe deeper, walk slower, and form genuine connections with the mountains and each other.

From sacred Jyotirlinga pilgrimages like Kedarnath to snow-covered summit ascents like Kedarkantha and Bali Pass, every route is vetted by our own founders before we take our first traveller batch.
