Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a trek?

You can book by sending us a WhatsApp message, filling out this form, or calling us directly. We'll confirm availability and guide you through the process.

What's included in the package price?

Most packages include accommodation, meals, guide fees, permits, and transportation from the base camp. Specific inclusions vary by package — check individual pages for details.

Can I customize a trip?

Absolutely! We specialize in custom trips for families, groups, and corporate teams. Tell us your preferences and we'll design the perfect itinerary.

What's the cancellation policy?

We offer flexible cancellation. Full refund if cancelled 15+ days before departure, 50% refund for 7-14 days, and case-by-case for shorter notice.
